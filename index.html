Our Story & Modest Heritage

The Parisian Modest House

Founded in 2014 by designer Myriam Atallah alongside her mother, JENNAH is a Parisian modest fashion house that harmonizes architectural minimalism with refined modest sensibilities.

Each collection represents a poetic balance between the designer's North African heritage (Tunisia and Algeria) and the understated sophistication of Parisian Haute Couture. Every cut celebrates silhouette fluidity, non-transparent opacity, and confident womanhood.

Durability and fabric integrity remain the cornerstone of our brand. We partner with ethical artisan workshops across Turkey and Portugal, hand-selecting certified organic cotton, breathable modal, and Medina silk.

Ethical Atelier Craftsmanship & Charity

In 2018, JENNAH opened its first flagship boutique in Aubervilliers, followed in 2024 by our concept store in Noisy-le-Sec. These physical spaces welcome women into a tranquil sanctuary with dedicated modest stylists ready to assist with sizing and layering.

Beyond fashion, JENNAH is deeply committed to humanitarian solidarity, donating a portion of annual proceeds to charitable initiatives supporting educational empowerment and clean water projects.
