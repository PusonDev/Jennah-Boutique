Our Story & Heritage

The Parisian Modest House

Founded in 2014 by designer Myriam Atallah alongside her mother, JENNAH is a Parisian modest fashion house that harmonizes architectural minimalism with refined modest sensibilities.

Each collection represents a poetic balance between the designer's North African heritage (Tunisia and Algeria) and the understated sophistication of Parisian Haute Couture. Every cut celebrates silhouette fluidity, modesty, and confident womanhood.

Durability and fabric integrity remain the cornerstone of our brand. We partner with ethical artisan workshops across Turkey, Asia, and Portugal, hand-selecting certified organic cotton, breathable modal, and fluid linens.

The Boutique Experience

In 2018, JENNAH opened its first flagship boutique in Aubervilliers, followed in 2024 by our second concept store in Noisy-le-Sec. These physical spaces welcome women into a tranquil sanctuary with dedicated stylists ready to assist with fit and layering.

Beyond fashion, JENNAH is deeply committed to humanitarian solidarity, donating a portion of annual proceeds to charities supporting underprivileged communities and educational empowerment.
