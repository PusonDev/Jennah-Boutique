Our story

The brand

Founded in 2014 by Myriam Atallah and her mother, JENNAH is a brand that combines refinement and minimalism in the field of modest fashion.

The collections reflect a unique fusion between the designer's Tunisian and Algerian heritage and the inspiration drawn from her Parisian environment. Each collection is a tribute to this cultural duality.

Each piece embodies timelessness, alternating between traditional designs for capsule collections and streamlined creations, inspired by Parisian elegance, for more essential collections.

Durability and fabric quality are at the heart of JENNAH's values.

The brand is committed to offering pieces designed to last, collaborating with the finest workshops in Turkey, Asia, and Europe, while prioritizing premium materials such as linen and cotton.

The JENNAH Experience

In 2018, JENNAH opened its first boutique in Aubervilliers, followed in 2024 by a second in Noisy-le-Sec. These boutiques offer a personalized experience, thanks to a team of experienced advisors ready to assist each customer in her choices.

In parallel, JENNAH is actively involved in social causes, donating a portion of its profits to charities working for the most disadvantaged.

With an international ambition, JENNAH continues its expansion, driven by values of quality, sustainability and timeless elegance.
